ePoster Display Modules


Sharp’s ePoster display modules offer an ultra-low-power, paper-replacement alternative to the expense and material waste of poster printing. Now, we’re introducing a new ISO A1 poster-size display module (40.5-inch diagonal) to complement our flagship A2-sized (28.5-inch diagonal) module, expanding opportunities for product developers.

Other products available soon from our ePoster roadmap include an A3-sized (20.2-inch diagonal) module and the outdoor A1 KaleidoTM3 (40.5-inch diagonal) module. Visit our News page for product release updates.
